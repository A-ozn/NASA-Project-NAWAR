Information Gaps & Local Review

NAWAR GeoAI supports rather than replaces farmer judgment. Verify these critical field conditions locally before planting:

    Satellite vs. Ground Truth: NAWAR Field Node

    NASA POWER climatology grids (~55 km) and SMAP pixels (9–36 km) capture regional climate but miss field-level microclimates. Our low-cost NAWAR Field Node soil moisture & temperature probe bridges that last-meter gap.

    1. Satellite Grid (~55 km)

    NASA POWER & SRTM provide regional temperature, rainfall, solar radiation, and elevation baselines.

    2. NAWAR Field Node

    In-field root-zone probe logs real soil moisture (%) and canopy-level temperature every 15 minutes.

    3. Calibrated Plan

    Sensor residuals bias-correct satellite ET and trigger localized irrigation and heat-stress alerts.
